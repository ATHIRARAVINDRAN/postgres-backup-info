Terms of use

PostgreSQL Backups is a personal utility for the owner's databases and Google Drive account. This website provides information about that utility.

Authorized use

The operator must have permission to access and back up the databases and Google Drive account they connect. This application is not offered as a public hosted backup service, and these pages do not provide database access.

Backup and recovery

Scheduled backups, encryption, checks, and restore tests reduce the risk of data loss, but they do not guarantee uninterrupted service or complete recovery in every situation. Work performed after the last successful backup may not be recoverable.

The owner is responsible for protecting credentials and retaining an independent copy of the recovery information and encryption keys. An encrypted backup cannot be recovered without its required key. Backup availability also depends on the owner's devices, network, Google account, and available storage.

Stopping use

The owner may stop the scheduled job and revoke the app's access in Google Account settings at any time. Removing authorization does not delete saved backups; the backup folder in Google Drive must be deleted separately if desired.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy describes the app's limited Google Drive access and data handling. Google Drive and GitHub Pages remain subject to their respective service terms and policies. This personal utility is provided as available, without a service-level guarantee.
